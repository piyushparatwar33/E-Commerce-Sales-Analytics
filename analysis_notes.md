# Excel Analysis Notes

Use `data/sales_data.csv` in Excel.

Recommended Pivot Tables:
1. Revenue and Profit by Month
2. Revenue and Profit by Category
3. Revenue by Region
4. Revenue by Customer Segment
5. Orders by Payment Method

Recommended KPI cells:
- Total Revenue: SUM(Revenue)
- Total Profit: SUM(Profit)
- Orders: DISTINCT COUNT of Order_ID
- AOV: Revenue / Orders
- Profit Margin: Profit / Revenue
